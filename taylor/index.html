Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Taylor

Many premature tank failures start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Drain and flush the tank every year to clear sediment, have the anode rod inspected every few years, and test the relief valve each year so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard to protect the heat exchanger.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing warning signs such as discolored hot water, rumbling during heating, moisture around the base, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Left alone, a small problem tends to grow. Take a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but once it's gone, the water starts eating the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a flooded utility room.

Common Water Heater Problems in Taylor

Every type of water heater has its own weak points, and we work on all of them around Taylor. Gas tank heaters usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units lose elements and thermostats, especially when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. Our technicians work on all four every week and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
