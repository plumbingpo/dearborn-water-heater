Kitchen Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Few businesses work a water heater harder than a restaurant. Every sink, sprayer, and dish cycle draws from the tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and a high-temperature dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Dearborn Water Heater services, sizes, and repairs the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we plan visits around your hours.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. Our technicians verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and log every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, keeping downtime short, and keeping part of the system online whenever the layout allows.

Heavy-Use Systems

Scale changes everything. One failed tank in a hotel can leave an entire floor with lukewarm showers, and a multifamily building with a single central heater has no margin for error. Dearborn Water Heater provides the know-how and structure these systems require, from recirculation pumps and mixing valves to staged heater banks.

Downtime in a large building is measured in refunds and tenant complaints, so our approach is built around prevention. If a unit does go down, our water heater repair technicians isolate it so the rest of the system keeps running.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, confirm adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

After-Hours Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Dearborn Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Dearborn and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get hot water flowing again, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Ongoing Protection

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Dearborn Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water quality, usage patterns, and budget.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
